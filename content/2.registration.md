# Join the Woodmont Civic Association

Any adult living in Woodmont can become a member by paying dues ($20 per household, per year) and being approved by the board.

Sign up with your contact details, then pay your dues with the same email address so we can match your payment. Once we've matched it, a board member will reach out to welcome you and confirm your membership. Here's [how to get started](/welcome/) in the meantime.
