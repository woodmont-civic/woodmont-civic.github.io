::home-section{id="what-we-do" title="What we do" tone="cream"}
The Woodmont Civic Association has served the neighborhood since the 1960s. It's run by volunteers and funded by neighbors, and it comes down to three things.

  ::help-list
    ::help-item{title="Get together"}
    Easy, low-key gatherings like socials, National Night Out, and, as the park comes together, music nights there. Come as you are, bring the kids, and meet the people next door.
    ::

    ::help-item{title="Take care of Woodmont"}
    Lighting, landscaping, and signs at our main entrances on Shoreham, Penrose, and Woodmont Drive, plus a warm welcome for new neighbors.
    ::

    ::help-item{title="Support the new park"}
    We're cheering on Friends of Woodmont Park as neighbors bring the old courts, field, and trails behind the pool back to life.
    ::
  ::
::

::home-section{id="park" title="A new gathering place" tone="cream"}
Woodmont has always been at its best when neighbors have somewhere to come together. Right now, a group of neighbors is turning the overgrown land behind the pool into that kind of place.

**Friends of Woodmont Park** is turning the old tennis courts into a multi-use court, clearing the field, and caring for the wooded trails. It's a separate nonprofit run by volunteers, working with the Woodmont Recreation Association, which owns the land.

The civic association is glad to be a neighbor and a supporter. As the park takes shape, we hope it becomes home to many of our gatherings: blankets on the field, music on the courts, and plenty of room for kids to run.

[See what's happening at the park](https://woodmontfriends.org/projects/)

::home-section{id="about" title="About the association" tone="cream"}
The purpose of the Woodmont Civic Association, in the words of our charter, is **"to unite the citizens of Woodmont in all civic efforts,"** with such efforts being directed toward making this community a better place in which to live. It began as a chartered corporation with an elected, volunteer Board of Directors, and today it's a voluntary neighborhood association.

Any adult who lives in Woodmont may become a member upon payment of dues and approval of the Board of Directors.

Woodmont sits in the Bon Air area of North Chesterfield, Virginia, along **Huguenot Road**, with entrances on Penrose, Shoreham, Dillion, Dolfield, Woodmont, and Astoria. St. Edward Epiphany School, St. Edward Catholic Church, and Huguenot Road Baptist Church line our northern boundary.

Please take a moment to read our [Neighborhood Covenant](https://github.com/woodmont-civic/woodmont-civic.github.io/releases/latest/download/Woodmont-Neighborhood-Covenant.pdf).
