# Neighborhood Covenant

The Woodmont Neighborhood Covenant outlines the community guidelines and rules that help maintain our beautiful and harmonious neighborhood. All residents should be familiar with these guidelines.

## About

The covenant is an important document that establishes:

- **Property maintenance standards** to keep our neighborhood looking its best
- **Architectural guidelines** for new construction and renovations  
- **Community expectations** for all residents
- **Guidelines for enforcement** to ensure everyone can enjoy living in Woodmont

## Who Should Read This?

Whether you're a **new resident** or have lived in Woodmont for years, familiarizing yourself with the covenant is essential. It ensures that:

- All residents understand community standards
- Property values are protected
- Our neighborhood maintains its character and appeal
- We can all work together to preserve what makes Woodmont special

## Questions?

If you have questions about the covenant or need clarification on any guidelines, please contact the Woodmont Civic Association Board of Directors.
