# Implementation Plan: "Welcome to Woodmont" Distribution Campaign

## Goal
Execute a multi-channel communication campaign to recruit volunteers ("Block Captains") for the "Welcome Basket" distribution event, aiming to increase neighborhood engagement and deliver 37+ baskets to new residents.

## Goal Description
We need to turn the next Board Meeting into a "Community Action Night." This plan covers the creation of digital and physical assets to support this event, ensuring clear messaging across Email, Facebook, and Nextdoor.

## Key Dates
- **Event Date**: TBD (Next Meeting)
- **Campaign Launch**: 2 Weeks prior to event.

## Proposed Components

### 1. Messaging Assets (Digital)
Located in `campaign_planning/marketing_copy/`
- **Email Blast**: Formatted for Mailchimp/Email list.
    - *Hook*: "37 New Neighbors need a warm welcome."
    - *Action*: "Pick up a basket, meet a neighbor."
- **Social Media Posts (FB/Nextdoor)**: 
    - Shorter, visual-heavy.
    - *Visual*: Photo of baskets (to be taken) or AI mockup.
- **Website Banner**: potentially add an alert to `index.md`?

### 2. Physical Assets (The Packet)
Located in `campaign_planning/print_assets/`
- **"Connect Card" (Quarter Sheet)**:
    - QR Code pointing to `woodmontbonair.com`.
    - "Pay Dues" generic link.
    - "Join the Mailing List" link.
    - *Why*: Bridges the physical basket to the digital infrastructure.

### 3. Verification & execution
- **Draft Review**: Board to review tone.
- **Mockups**: Create visual mockups of the flyer/card.
